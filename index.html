About me

I'm a junior full-stack developer who took the scenic route into tech. For four years I designed and assembled modern kitchens at ACS Cozinhas, drawing every cabinet and countertop in 3D with Teowin, a CAD program made for exactly that. Turns out "measure twice, cut once" is also a pretty good rule for writing code.

Since then I've earned a Computer Programming diploma at IEFP Lisbon and done internships at Fnac, Siemens and Teamlyzer, where I shipped features and fixed bugs in a production Python/Flask codebase. I still care about the same things I did as a designer: a solid structure, details that fit, and a client who's happy with the result.

Kitchens or code, I build things people use every day.
