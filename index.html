CentiSible

Personal and household finance app, full-stack and installable as a PWA.

  • 231 backend tests
  • 9 E2E tests
  • 6 CI jobs green
  • 10 friends testing it

01 The problem

Spreadsheets at 11pm trying to work out where the money went. And for couples, shared costs like rent counted twice, once for each person.

02 What I built

Accounts, transactions, budgets, savings goals and recurring expenses, with a dashboard that flags what needs attention. A household view merges two people's finances and counts shared expenses once.

03 Decisions worth explaining

  • Money is never a float. Amounts are PostgreSQL NUMERIC, so cents don't drift.
  • Layers with one job each. Router → service → repository: business rules are tested without HTTP or UI.
  • A real production bug. Sessions died right after login: the refresh cookie was SameSite=Lax, which browsers don't send between the frontend and API domains. Fixed with SameSite=None.
